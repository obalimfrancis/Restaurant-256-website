A Taste of Home

Crafting Comfort Since 2015 Mr.Tasty was born from a simple belief: that the best meals are those that make you feel at home. Founded in 2015, we have become a neighborhood staple for those seeking honest, flavor-packed comfort food in the heart of the city. Our kitchen is led by Chef Leo Mubiru, who spent years mastering the art of the perfect bite. One delicious plate at a time.
