Our Story

A taste of home, crafted since 2015

Mr. Tasty was born from a simple belief: the best meals are the ones that make you feel at home. We opened with eight tables, one grill and a short menu we were stubborn about.

A decade on, we are a neighbourhood staple for anyone after honest, flavour-packed comfort food in the heart of the city. Our kitchen is led by Chef Leo Mubiru, who spent years mastering the art of the perfect bite — one delicious plate at a time.
